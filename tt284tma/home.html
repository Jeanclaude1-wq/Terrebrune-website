Terrebrune is a destination hotel for four seasons

Timeless sophistication intertwined with the symphony of natural beauty

Situated in the heart of the Mzaar ski resort. Four stars Terre Brune Hotel is situated on Faqra Main Road in Kfardebian only in 1.9 km from the centre. It is ideally acceptable for a mountains, ski/wintersports vacation. The hotel is only in 35.7 km from the Rafic Hariri Airport. Upon arrival, Terrebrune Hotel invites guests into a realm of opulent luxury, where meticulously curated communal areas and guest rooms seamlessly blend with the breathtaking surroundings, evoking an atmosphere of enchantment. Embark on an elegant journey at Terrebrune, where each moment unfolds as a captivating exploration of a majestic environment, waiting to be discovered.
